How the silver moves

  1. 01
    It really turns

    The ewer’s body is mapped around its own axis, so the engraving rotates while the handle swings. The studio shading stays still, the way real light behaves.

  2. 02
    It catches your light

    Surface relief is read from the photographs, so highlights travel across the filigree as you move. The silver also picks up the page’s colours at its edges.

  3. 03
    The two pieces reflect each other

    The basin’s polished centre reflects the ewer standing in front of it, and the ewer’s edges pick up the basin. Drop in GLB models later and the same choreography carries over.
